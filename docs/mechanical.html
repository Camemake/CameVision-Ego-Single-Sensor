Where the heat is

Thermal path: RV1126B. One ISP and the NPU. The camera, eMMC and VS6621 stay clear of that path.

One camera draws less than Ego’s dual ISP. Still design the lid pad for the NPU-on band in still air.

Cooling is part of the housing

1. Pick up the lid A 1–2 mm isolating silicone gap pad on the RV1126B lid, compressed 10–30 % onto aluminium or steel.
2. Use the via field A second pad on the connector-side via field, into the same metal.
3. Hold the board, not the barrel Bosses or a pocket under the outline. The camera floats in its hole.
4. Leave service faces USB-C, BOOT, RST and PH stay reachable. Maskrom: hold BOOT, tap RST.

Assembly

1

Board

  1. Handle the outline, not the barrel and not the SoC lid.
  2. Seat the MIPI / module connector fully. A half-click gives 0 fps and CSI errors.
2

Thermal parts

  1. Lid pad on the RV1126B square only.
  2. Back pad on the via field only. Stay off USB-C, PH, RST, BOOT.
3

Housing

  1. Lower the board onto the bosses. The camera enters its hole last, with no side load.
  2. Close the lid so the SoC pad compresses. Check that BOOT and RST still click.
