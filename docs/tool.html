USB

Maskrom

  1. Connect USB-C and power the board.
  2. Install the USB driver that ships in this package.
  3. Hold BOOT, tap RESET, keep BOOT held.
  4. When the log shows Maskrom or Loader, continue.

Package

Install

Writes loader, env, idblock, U-Boot, boot, userdata, oem, rootfs, and the overlay.

Release BOOT when the write finishes and the board starts.

ADB

Open

USB forwards and the live page at http://127.0.0.1:8081/.

Use the camera

After install, the board has three options. The last two are the phone app (android/).

1 · Camemake UVC Plug USB and open Windows Camera. Full resolution. No phone. Mono binds this gadget after Wi-Fi is up.
2 · Standalone Pair CameVision Ego Single + last 6 MAC hex. Record on the card over Bluetooth. Send through the phone to the server in Setup. Clear the card from the app. Position preview is 10 seconds at quarter resolution.
3 · Server Same app. The camera uploads over its Wi-Fi. Replace the template https://server.example.com/camevision/v1 in Setup. Leave the token empty when that server is open.

Card files: camevision/sessions/<UTC>/session.json, imu0.csv, cam0/video.mp4. App: CameVision 0.1. UVC: Camemake 2207:0016 MJPEG 1400×1600 and the SD card as a drive.
